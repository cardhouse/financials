# Vision

## Product Summary

This project is a local-first household finance planning application for a two-person household. Its purpose is to give both household members a shared, trustworthy view of their financial situation and help them answer the practical question:

> Are we on track this month?

The application should provide visibility into income, debt, account balances, monthly bills, budget progress, savings goals, and upcoming financial obligations. It should also help the household understand the consequences of financial decisions before making them.

## Core Problem

Most household financial decisions are not made from a single complete view. Account balances, bills, debt, savings goals, and spending categories are often spread across bank portals, credit card apps, spreadsheets, memory, and conversations.

This app should bring those pieces together so the household can quickly understand:

- Whether the current month is on track.
- Which categories are over or approaching budget.
- What bills are coming due.
- How much cash is truly available to spend.
- How choices affect debt, goals, and future cash flow.

## Primary Outcome

The primary output of the system is confidence.

A successful version of this app should let the household open the dashboard and quickly know one of the following:

- We are on track this month.
- We are spending too much in one or more categories.
- We are overallocated and need to adjust.
- A planned purchase is affordable.
- A planned purchase requires trade-offs.

## Product Identity

This is not intended to be traditional accounting software. It is also not only a transaction tracker.

It is a household financial operating system focused on:

- Understanding the current month.
- Planning future months.
- Testing financial scenarios.
- Making trade-offs visible.
- Keeping financial data local and under household control.

## Guiding Question

Every major feature should help answer:

> Given where we are financially today, what is the best thing to do next?

## Initial Scope

The first version should focus on manual entry and high-value visibility rather than automation.

V1 should support:

- Two equal-permission users in one household.
- Manual account balance tracking.
- Expected monthly income plus one-off income.
- Monthly category totals and quick purchase entry.
- Fixed expenses, flexible spending, and savings/sinking funds.
- Bills with due dates and paid/unpaid status.
- Budget progress and available-to-spend calculations.
- Scenario planning with temporary and saved potential budgets.
- Basic debt and goal visibility.

## Long-Term Direction

The application should be designed so future versions can add:

- CSV transaction imports.
- Optional bank or credit card integrations.
- Local LLM-generated financial insights.
- Debt payoff strategy comparisons.
- Goal completion forecasting.
- More advanced reporting and visualizations.

No future feature should require giving up local control of financial data.
